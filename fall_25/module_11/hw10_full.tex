        end_point = torch.randn(1, latent_dim).to(device)

        # This creates a smooth transition vector
        alphas = torch.linspace(0, 1, num_steps).to(device).unsqueeze(1).unsqueeze(2)

\subsubsection*{Solution 2 (a)}

To construct the 100-dimensional embeddings, the Positive Pointwise Mutual Information factorization approach was applied to the Brown corpus from the NLTK library. The steps are outlined below:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Vocabulary ($V$) and Context ($C$):} The top 5,000 most frequent words from the corpus to form the vocabulary $V$. For the context words $C$, the top 1,000 most frequent words were selected
    \item \textbf{Window Definition:} The context window was defined as a symmetric window of size 2 (i.e., 2 words preceding and 2 words succeeding the target word).
    \item \textbf{Co-occurrence Matrix Construction:} A $5000 \times 1000$ raw count matrix $M$ where $M_{ij}$ represents the number of times context word $c_j$ appeared within the window of target word $w_i$.
    \item \textbf{PPMI Reweighting:} $M$ was converted to a PPMI matrix where each entry is calculated as $\max(\log(\frac{P(w,c)}{P(w)P(c)}), 0)$. This calculation reweights the entries to represent the likelihood that two words are to co-occur than by random chance.
    \item \textbf{Dimensionality Reduction (SVD):} Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) on the sparse PPMI matrix to obtain the rank-100 approximation: $M_{PPMI} \approx U \Sigma V^T$. 
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection*{\normalfont}{$\therefore$ The $\mathbb{R}^{100}$ is just the representation of the dimensionality reduction of the original $\mathbb{R}^{5000}$ space.}

\subsubsection*{Solution 2 (c)}

The K-Means Clustering algorithm to group the 100-dimensional embeddings into $k=100$ clusters. 

\subsubsection*{Algorithm and Distance Choice}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Algorithm: K-Means.} I chose K-Means because it is computationally efficient for a vocabulary size of $N=5000$ and it produces hard partitions, which simplifies the qualitative analysis of the word groups.
    \item \textbf{Distance Function: Euclidean (on L2-normalized vectors).} Standard K-Means optimizes for Euclidean distance. Euclidean distance and Cosine distance are monotonically related by:
    $$||x-y||^2 = 2(1 - \cos\theta)$$
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection*{Cluster Analysis:}
The resulting clusters exhibit high coherence, below are examples of this:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Cluster 1 (Time/Duration):} \textit{day, year, week, month, hour, moment, night, morning}
    \item \textbf{Cluster 2 (Family/People):} \textit{mother, father, son, daughter, wife, husband, girl, boy}
    \item \textbf{Cluster 3 (Prepositions/Grammar):} \textit{in, on, at, from, by, with, for}
    \item \textbf{Cluster 4 (Politics/Civics):} \textit{government, state, nation, country, federal, law, policy}
\end{itemize}
